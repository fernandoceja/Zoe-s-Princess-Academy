# Zoe's Starlight Quest — Learning Standards

> Parent Corner source document. Each of the four quests maps to a California
> 1st-grade standard. No scores, no penalties — wrong answers get a gentle hint
> and a retry.

## 🌸 Belle — "The Torn Storybook" · Whispering Library

- **Standard:** ELA Reading Foundations — CA CCSS **RF.1.3 / RF.1.4**
- **What the child practices:** Phonics and sight-word recognition — decoding
  short-vowel (CVC) words and recognizing common Dolch first-grade words.
- **Example round:** *"Sound it out: c - a - t. Which word is it?"*
  — choices: `cat`, `cup`, `cap`.

## ❄️ Elsa — "The Frozen Bridge" · Frostfall Peaks

- **Standard:** Math, Operations within 20 — CA CCSS **1.OA**
- **What the child practices:** Adding and subtracting within 20 (sums ≤ 20,
  minuends ≤ 20, no negative answers), with countable on-screen snowflakes as
  visual supports. Also counting on from the greater addend, 1 to 3 (CA CCSS
  **1.OA.5 / 1.OA.6**, school Lesson 3.2) and doubles 1+1 through 5+5
  (**1.OA.6**, Lesson 3.3).
- **Example round:** *"The bridge needs 7 + 5 snowflakes. How many?"*
  — choices: `12`, `11`, `13`.

## 🌻 Rapunzel — "The Sleepy Garden" · Sunflower Meadows

- **Standard:** Science: what plants need / living vs. nonliving — **NGSS 1-LS1** (CA);
  Math: measuring — **CA CCSS 1.MD.1–2**
- **What the child practices:** What plants need to grow (water, sunlight,
  soil) and distinguishing living things from nonliving things. Also
  measuring: ordering and comparing flower heights, and counting petal units
  for a stem's length. Sorting bins: tap a picture into Living or Not living
  (living things need food and water, NGSS K-LS1-1 style).
- **Example round:** *"A sleepy seed wants to wake up and grow. What does it drink first?"*
  — choices: `Water`, `Juice`, `Milk`.

## 🕰️ Cinderella — "The Midnight Clock" · Glass Slipper Village

- **Standard:** Math, telling time — CA CCSS **1.MD.3**
- **What the child practices:** Reading an analog clock to the hour and the
  half-hour.
- **Example round:** *"What time is the clock showing?"* (clock shows 5:30)
  — choices: `half past 5`, `5 o'clock`, `half past 6`.

## 🐚 Ariel — "The Seashell Songs" · Seashell Cove

- **Standard:** ELA Reading Foundations — CA CCSS **RF.1.2 / RF.1.3**
- **What the child practices:** Rhyming and word families with short CVC words
  (-at, -an, -ap, -en, -et, -ig, -in, -it, -og, -op, -ot, -ug, -un). Easy: which word or pair rhymes.
  Core: pick the rhyme, find the word that does not rhyme. Stretch: add a letter to a family ending to make a new word.
- **Example round:** *"Which word rhymes with pig?"* — choices: `wig`, `pan`, `bug`.

## 🏝️ Moana — "The Shape Stones" · Wayfinder Isle

- **Standard:** Math Geometry — CA CCSS **1.G.A.1 / 1.G.A.2** (and K.G)
- **What the child practices:** Defining attributes, not color, size or turn. Easy: name a shape picture (circle, triangle, square, rectangle, hexagon; cube, sphere, cone, cylinder).
  Core: count sides and corners of flat shapes, find the shape with no corners or a given count (pentagon joins in).
  Stretch: solid shapes and real things (a ball, a dice, a can), flat faces of solids, which thing can roll.
- **Example round:** *"How many sides does a triangle have?"* — choices: `3`, `4`, `5`.

## 🏮 Jasmine — "The Story Lanterns" · Moonlit Palace Garden

- **Standard:** ELA Reading Literature — CA CCSS **RL.1.2** (retell stories with key details), RL.K.2 and RL.1.3
- **What the child practices:** Putting a tiny 3-step story in order with first / next / last (beginning, middle, end), from everyday sequences she knows.
  Easy: which picture comes first. Core: what happens last, what comes in the middle, what happens next after the first two pictures.
  Stretch: pick the right order ("first sprout, then plant, last flower") or listen to a three-sentence story and say what happened first or last.
- **Example round:** *"What comes first?"* with three picture cards (a chick, an egg, a hen) — choices: `egg`, `baby chick`, `big hen`.

## ⚓ Anna — "The Harbor Signs" · Sunny Fjord Harbor

- **Standard:** ELA Language — CA CCSS **L.1.2.a** (capitalize dates and names of people), **L.1.2.b** (end punctuation), and L.K.2.a (capitalize the first word and the pronoun I)
- **What the child practices:** Fixing short sentences on harbor signs.
  Easy: which mark ends the sentence (period for telling, question mark for asking, exclamation mark for excited).
  Core: which word needs a capital letter in a sentence written in lowercase (the first word, a name like Zoe or Sam, or the word I).
  Stretch: which of three versions of a sentence is written the right way (capital letter plus the right end mark).
  Only clear-cut sentences are used, so a period and an exclamation mark are never both right.
- **Example round:** *"Which mark ends this sentence?"* showing `Where is the cat?` — choices: `. period`, `? question mark`, `! exclamation mark`.
